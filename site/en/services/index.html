How an engagement runs

Every engagement starts with a scoping call and a short written scope: which licence or regime applies, what the regulator will expect, what you already have, and what we'll produce. You get a fixed scope and a named engagement lead before any work starts.

Deliverables are prepared in Arabic and English. Where a requirement is unclear in the published rules, we say so in writing and tell you how we propose to handle it with the regulator.

Who we work with

  • Foreign fintechs and payment companies entering Saudi Arabia
  • Saudi founders preparing a first SAMA or CMA application
  • Licensed payment, BNPL, finance and crowdfunding companies that need AML or compliance support
  • CMA-authorised firms and FinTech Lab participants
  • Crypto and web3 firms that need to understand the Saudi perimeter or license elsewhere in the GCC

Last reviewed: 11 October 2026

General information, not legal advice. Saudi rules change; we confirm every requirement against the regulator's current text before you file.
