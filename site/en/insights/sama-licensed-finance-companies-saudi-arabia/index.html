What SAMA's register shows about finance licences in 2025 and 2026

SAMA put licensed finance companies at 80 on 22 Nov 2026, up from 62 at end-2024. BNPL and consumer microfinance led new licences. Register data.

اقرأ بالعربية

As of 20 November 2026, SAMA's register of licensed finance entities lists 78 finance companies and 13 finance support companies. SAMA's own announcements put the number of licensed finance companies at 62 on 31 December 2024 and 78 on 30 August 2026. Of the 22 finance company licences SAMA announced between January 2025 and November 2026, 15 went to buy now, pay later (BNPL) and consumer microfinance firms.

Key takeaways

Nine companies on the register hold BNPL as an activity, five of them as their only activity. Fifteen list debt-based crowdfunding, and 13 list one of the two consumer microfinance activities. SAMA announced seven finance aggregation licences over the same period, and the register now lists 11 aggregators. No finance company licence was announced between 30 August and 22 November 2026, when SAMA announced two (see the update below).

Update, 23 November 2026

On 22 November 2026 SAMA announced two more finance company licences. It licensed Neo for consumer microfinance through financial technology, putting the total at 79, and then licensed Izdihar Financing Company for SME finance, putting it at 80. The Arabic releases give the same totals. When we checked on 23 November 2026, the Finance register still showed 91 entries and did not yet list either company. The tables and counts below are our 20 November 2026 snapshot and cover announcements up to that date.

How we counted

We read two SAMA sources on 20 November 2026. The first is the Finance page of SAMA's Licensed Entities register, which shows each company's name, its category (finance company or finance support company), the activities it is licensed for, its unified number and its licence number. We captured all 91 entries. The second is SAMA's news page, where each new finance licence is announced, usually with a running total of licensed finance companies. We read every announcement from 31 December 2024 to 20 November 2026 and checked a sample of the Arabic versions, which give the same numbers.

A company licensed for several activities appears once on the register but counts once in each activity row of the table below, so the rows add up to more than 91.

Leaving aside the December 2024 baseline, that is 22 announcements: 11 in 2025 and 11 in 2026 up to 20 November. By activity, seven were for BNPL (including Tamara and Tabby, whose register entries list other activities too), eight consumer microfinance (four of them through financial technology), one microfinance, two debt crowdfunding, two consumer finance and two multi-activity lenders.

The stated total rose by 16 while 22 licences were announced. Five announcements left the total unchanged or lowered it, and the releases do not say why. One exit is on record: on 13 November 2025 SAMA announced that it had revoked the licence of Fas Finance Company, which was licensed for consumer microfinance through financial technology, after the company asked for cancellation on completing its liquidation; the release says the owner decided on 10 July 2025 to liquidate it as a dormant company. That revocation does not line up with a fall in the totals SAMA stated. The 78 finance companies on the 20 November 2026 register match the last total SAMA published.

Checks against SAMA's activity counts

Some releases also give the number of companies in one activity, which lets us test the register:

  • Debt crowdfunding: SAMA counted 12 companies on 31 December 2024 and 14 on 24 November 2025, although we found only one debt crowdfunding licence announced between those dates (Madd Balas). Mansat Hima followed in July 2026, and the register lists 15.
  • Finance aggregation: SAMA counted 5 aggregators on 23 March 2025 and has announced six more since. The register lists 11.
  • Consumer microfinance: SAMA counted 12 companies offering consumer microfinance on 28 January 2026, and 11 on 28 April 2026, when it licensed Tak Mal for consumer microfinance through financial technology. The Arabic release ties the 11 to that activity (هذا النشاط), so the two counts may cover different activities. SAMA then licensed Credit Line in July 2026. The register lists 13 under its two consumer microfinance activities. We found no release that explains these movements.

What the numbers mean for founders and compliance teams

These points are our reading of SAMA's data. SAMA's licence releases do not comment on its licensing pipeline.

  1. Consumer credit at the point of sale is where SAMA has been licensing. BNPL and the consumer microfinance activities took 15 of the 22 licences. Their capital tiers are SAR 5 million for BNPL only, and SAR 10 million or SAR 20 million for consumer microfinance, depending on whether the company uses financial technology only. A new BNPL entrant would join nine licensed BNPL providers, four of which are licensed for other finance activities as well. Capital follows the highest figure, so on our reading of Article 8 a company planning BNPL plus consumer microfinance (outside the financial technology only tier) should budget for SAR 20 million rather than the SAR 5 million BNPL-only figure, unless SAMA sets a different figure.
  2. SAMA announced about one finance company licence a month from January 2025 to August 2026. From 30 August to 22 November 2026 it announced none, the longest gap since January 2025, before licensing Neo and Izdihar on the same day. Do not build a launch plan on a fixed approval date. The BNPL licence page sets out the application steps.
  3. Aggregation is the main route for firms that want to sit between lenders and borrowers without lending themselves. Eleven aggregators are licensed, and SAMA announced seven of them from March 2025 onwards.
  4. Few new debt crowdfunding licences have been announced. SAMA's own count rose from 12 to 14 by November 2025, and the register now lists 15, but we found only two new debt crowdfunding licences announced from January 2025 to November 2026.
  5. Licences end as well as begin. A partner, lender or merchant counterparty you rely on today may leave the register, so check it at onboarding and again at each contract renewal.

Checks to run before you file or sign

  • Before choosing an activity, map your product to the activities SAMA lists on its register, and plan for the capital of the highest one your product touches.
  • Before you sign with a lender, BNPL provider or aggregator, find it on SAMA's Finance register and record the licence number and listed activities in your due diligence file. SAMA's own releases tell the public to deal only with authorised institutions and to check its website.
  • If your product overlaps with BNPL, consumer microfinance or aggregation, filter SAMA's Finance register by that activity, look at the licensed firms and work out how your model differs before you file.
  • Recheck the register before any regulator meeting. We will compare our 20 November 2026 snapshot with later ones and update this guide when the count changes.
