E-money institution licence

Wallets and stored-value products that issue electronic money need SAMA's e-money institution licence, in a Micro or Major category.

What SAMA looks at closely

Safeguarding of customer funds, wallet limits and tiered KYC, agent and distributor arrangements, and transaction monitoring that works on real-time wallet flows.

The 2026 amendments to the AML Implementing Regulation explicitly address electronic wallets and e-money, and tighten beneficial-ownership checks. Your AML programme should be written against the amended text.

What we deliver

  • Category recommendation based on projected float and volume
  • Safeguarding, wallet-limit and KYC tiering design
  • Full application file and policies in Arabic and English
  • AML/CFT programme for wallet flows, including sanctions screening and monitoring scenarios
  • Support through SAMA's questions, approval in principle and conditions
