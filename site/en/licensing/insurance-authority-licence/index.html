Insurance Authority licensing for insurtechs

The Insurance Authority (IA) has regulated insurance in Saudi Arabia since 23 November 2023, taking over the mandate SAMA used to hold. Insurers, brokers, aggregators and embedded-insurance models now plan their licence around the IA.

The Insurance Authority took over from SAMA in November 2023

The IA was created under its own Statute, approved by Council of Ministers Resolution No. 85 dated 28/1/1445H (August 2023). Law-firm commentary describes it as reporting to the Prime Minister, with financial and administrative independence. The IA's website states that it became the authorised insurance regulator from 23 November 2023, and SAMA's insurance mandate moved across in that transition. Health insurance mandates followed from the Council of Health Insurance on 4 March 2024, according to the same commentary.

The core sector law has not changed yet. It is still the Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law, Royal Decree M/32 dated 2/6/1424H (31 July 2003), amended by Royal Decrees M/30 and M/12, with Implementing Regulations issued by Minister of Finance Decision 1/596. The Cooperative Health Insurance Law of 1999 now sits with the IA as well.

Rules issued in the SAMA era stay in force until the IA replaces them. That is why several instruments an insurtech works with, including the insurance aggregation rules, are still published on the SAMA Rulebook. Insurance complaints have moved to the IA, while any payment activity in your product stays with SAMA.

The service-provider categories and the aggregator capital figures come from secondary sources. We have not confirmed whether the IA has re-issued the SAMA aggregation rules under its own name, so we check the current text before you plan capital.

Insurtech, aggregation and open insurance rules

The IA inherited a set of instruments written for online sales. Commentary refers to an Online Insurance Activities Regulation from 2011 and insurtech rules issued in 2023. For comparison sites and e-brokers the aggregation rules matter most: they decide who may compare and sell policies online and on what capital base.

A draft Open Insurance and Insurtech Regulations was published in 2025, covering API data-sharing and digital distribution, according to Al Tamimi. We have not seen it finalised. If your product depends on pulling policy or customer data from insurers through APIs, design for that draft and keep the data model consistent with the Personal Data Protection Law.

The market is still small. Reporting on the Financial Sector Development Program's 2024 annual report counted 14 IA-licensed fintechs at the end of 2024, against 138 licensed by SAMA and 52 by the CMA.

Testing first in the Insurance Lab

The IA launched its Insurance Regulatory Sandbox Framework, the Insurance Lab, in September 2024. The framework is published on the IA website and covers scope, target segments, phases, reporting, extension and exit.

The Lab suits a model that does not fit an existing licence category, or one that needs live customers to prove the concept. A sandbox place is time-limited and ends in exit or a licence application, so we write the test plan around the evidence the IA will want at the end: customer outcomes, complaints, incidents and the controls that worked.

The new Insurance Law is still a draft

A draft new Insurance Law went to public consultation until 22 July 2025. According to HFW, it would consolidate the 2003 law and the insurance provisions of the health insurance law. We found no evidence that it had been enacted as of October 2026.

Applications today are assessed under the current law and the rules carried over from SAMA. We build your file on those, and keep a short list of the draft provisions that would change your model so you are ready if the law is passed.

Insurance and payments are separate perimeters

Collecting premiums into a wallet or paying claims out to cards can bring SAMA payment licensing into the picture as well. We map both before choosing a structure. See the payment institution licence.

Common questions

Does SAMA still license insurance companies?

No. The Insurance Authority has been the insurance regulator since 23 November 2023. Some SAMA-era rules, including the insurance aggregation rules, are still on the SAMA Rulebook and stay in force until the IA replaces them.

Do we need an IA licence to sell insurance inside our app?

If your platform arranges, compares or sells policies, plan on IA licensing or on partnering with a licensed broker or aggregator. Embedded and digital distribution models sit inside the IA's perimeter.

Is the new Insurance Law in force?

We found no evidence that it has been enacted. The draft went to consultation until 22 July 2025. Plan on the 2003 Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law and its regulations.

Can an insurtech test before applying for a full licence?

Yes, through the Insurance Lab, the IA's regulatory sandbox launched in September 2024. A place is time-limited and leads to exit or a licence application.
