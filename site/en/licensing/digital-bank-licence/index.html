Digital bank licence

A digital-only bank in Saudi Arabia is licensed as a bank under the Banking Control Law, with SAMA's 2020 additional guidelines for digital-only banks applied on top of the 2018 bank licensing criteria. There is no published minimum capital: SAMA sets it case by case, and the most recent licensee, EZ Bank, was approved with SAR 2.5 billion.

The legal basis

Banking business in Saudi Arabia is governed by the Banking Control Law (Royal Decree M/5, 22/2/1386H). SAMA's Licensing Guidelines and Minimum Criteria of 1 December 2018 set the general requirements for a bank licence, and the Additional Licensing Guidelines and Criteria for Digital-Only Banks of 24 February 2020 adapt them for banks with no branch network. A digital-only bank meets the same prudential requirements as any other bank, and the banking consumer protection principles apply to it.

A new Banking Law to replace the Banking Control Law went to public consultation in early 2023, and law-firm and press reports say it was submitted to the legislative authorities in August 2025. We found no evidence that it had been enacted as of October 2026, so applications still run under the existing law and guidelines.

How the licensing process runs

  1. Pre-application consultation

    Discuss the proposal with SAMA before filing.

  2. Formal application

    Submit in hard copy and by email, signed by two authorised directors, including the ICAAP, ILAAP and the mandatory exit plan.

  3. Acknowledgement

    SAMA acknowledges receipt and names a case officer within 15 business days.

  4. Assessment

    SAMA reviews the application and may appoint an independent assessor for the design phase. The guidelines set no fixed decision deadline.

  5. Council of Ministers decision

    Bank licences are granted by Council of Ministers decision. EZ Bank's licence, for example, followed Cabinet approval announced by SAMA.

  6. Build and no-objection to commence

    Incorporate, build and test, with an implementation-phase assessment if SAMA requires one. Operations start only after a separate SAMA no-objection to commence.

What the capital figure tells you

SAMA does not publish a minimum, but the one official figure, SAR 2.5 billion for EZ Bank, shows the scale involved. The ICAAP and ILAAP drive the number, so the capital discussion depends on how well those assessments are built. Promoters must also show financial capacity.

For most fintechs a bank licence is more than the model needs. If the model does not need to take deposits, a payment institution, e-money or finance company licence has published capital figures and decision deadlines.

People and senior roles

Banks are within the scope of SAMA's Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions. Article 4 reserves roles such as the Chief Compliance Officer, AML/CTF Director, Anti-Fraud Director, IT Director and Information or Cyber Security Director for Saudi nationals, and senior appointments need SAMA's written non-objection before the person acts. The English rulebook page states it is not the latest version, so work from the Arabic text. See compliance function.

Common questions

What is the minimum capital for a digital bank in Saudi Arabia?

SAMA does not publish one. Capital is assessed case by case from the applicant's ICAAP and ILAAP. EZ Bank, licensed in 2025, was approved with SAR 2.5 billion.

Does a digital bank need branches?

No branches are expected, but the bank needs a head office in Saudi Arabia, and SAMA may require customer service centres.

Who grants the licence?

The Council of Ministers, on SAMA's assessment. Operations then need a separate SAMA no-objection to commence.

How long does SAMA take to decide?

SAMA acknowledges the application and names a case officer within 15 business days. The guidelines set no deadline for the decision itself.

Is there a new Banking Law?

A draft went to consultation in 2023 and was reportedly submitted to the legislative authorities in August 2025. We found no evidence of enactment as of October 2026.
