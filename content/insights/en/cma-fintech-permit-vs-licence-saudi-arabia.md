If your product is a securities activity the Capital Market Institutions Regulations already cover, such as arranging a crowdfunding offer, advising, or managing portfolios by algorithm, the CMA expects you to apply for a capital market institution licence. The FinTech Experimental Permit, issued through the CMA's FinTech Lab, is for testing an innovative model that has no settled licence route yet. It runs under conditions the CMA sets, for up to two years from the day you start business. As of 25 November 2026, the CMA's [list of financial market institutions](https://cma.gov.sa/en/Market/AuthorisedPersons/Pages/default.aspx) shows 245 licensed institutions and its [FinTech Lab list](https://cma.gov.sa/en/Market/fintech/Pages/ExpFinTechs.aspx) shows 45 permit companies.

> **Key takeaways** The permit is a test, and the Instructions say it is not a way to launch a product that can already comply with the full rules. The two most recent Lab models to move into licences are debt crowdfunding (Arranging, September 2025) and robo-advisory (Managing, March 2026). The date on the Lab list is the CMA Board's decision. The two-year clock starts later, at commencement of business, which in five CMA announcements we read came between about 10 months and just over two years after the decision. Seven companies on the Lab list also appear by name on the licensed list. A 2026 draft would bring the permit's entry tests closer to the licence tests; it is still a draft.

## Which route fits your product

Section 3 of the 2021 Instructions draws the line. A licensed institution that already holds the authorisation a product needs does not need a permit, and one that lacks it must apply to amend its licence. A product that already complies with the rules and is ready for the wider market goes straight to a licence application. Eligibility and the application steps are on our [FinTech Experimental Permit](@/licensing/cma-fintech-experimental-permit/) page.

The CMA has also closed the Lab to some models. Its FinTech Lab FAQ says the Lab does not currently receive equity crowdfunding applications and that firms can apply directly for a licence. For debt crowdfunding, the CMA said in September 2025 that it will no longer accept new Lab applications unless they involve an innovative aspect requiring testing.

| Your model | Route on the CMA's own statements (as of 25 November 2026) | Licence activity |
|---|---|---|
| Equity crowdfunding | Lab not receiving applications; apply for a licence (FAQ). See [crowdfunding](@/licensing/crowdfunding-licence-saudi-arabia/) | Arranging |
| Debt crowdfunding | Licence; Lab only for an innovative aspect (CMA_N_3862, 8 September 2025) | Arranging |
| Robo-advisory | Licence (CMA_N_3994, 5 March 2026). See [robo-advisory](@/licensing/robo-advisory-licence/) | Managing Investments, or Managing Investments and Operating Funds |
| Fund distribution platforms | Both routes exist: the CMA Glossary covers platforms set up by licensed financial market institutions and platforms holding a FinTech permit | Ask the CMA which licence your model needs |
| Social trading, AI in advisory, stock ownership transfer | On the live Lab list; we found no CMA statement moving these models into a licence | Ask the CMA which route applies |

A product aimed only at professional investors is not exempt. The FAQ says a securities-business fintech product needs a permit "regardless of the target customers".

All three debt-instrument names now hold an Arranging licence, the activity the CMA named for debt crowdfunding in September 2025. In the CMA's words, permit holders "will continue operating until the expiry of their permits. Afterwards, they may apply for the appropriate license".

Several of these licences are marked not yet commenced, so a licence on the list does not mean the licensed business is running yet. Afaq Capital's permit dates from November 2025, and the lists do not show when its licence was granted. A licensed institution may hold a permit too: under section 3(D) of the Instructions, a firm that considers the permit the only appropriate framework for testing its product can apply, and the CMA may accept or reject the application.

## The two-year clock starts later than the list date

The "License Date" on the Lab list matches the date of the CMA Board's decision. Each commencement announcement cites that decision: [CMA_N_4110](https://cma.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/NEWS/Pages/CMA_N_4110.aspx), for example, says Lamha Tanbu completed its commencement requirements "pursuant to the decision of the CMA's Board dated 14/05/2025G", the same date the Lab list shows. The permit's validity runs from commencement of business (section 10(A)), which the announcement clears the company to begin, so the two years start well after the list date.

| Company (model) | Board decision (as cited in the announcement) | Commencement requirements completed, as announced | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owais Al-Osool (debt instruments) | 8 July 2024 | 21 May 2025 | about 10 and a half months |
| Sahem Financial Company (debt instruments; the Lab list names its 8 July 2024 debt entry Sahem Financial Platform Company) | 8 July 2024 | 22 July 2025 | about 12 and a half months |
| InvestSky (social trading) | 5 February 2024 | 1 June 2025 | almost 16 months |
| Lamha Tanbu (AI in advisory) | 14 May 2025 | 31 August 2026 | about 15 and a half months |
| Sindbad.Tech (AI in advisory) | 27 November 2023 | 8 December 2025 | just over two years |

The gap is the commencement work in section 7 of the Instructions, which our [permit page](@/licensing/cma-fintech-experimental-permit/) lists in full. The FAQ adds that no legal entity is needed to apply, but after approval every applicant, foreign companies included, must set one up in the Kingdom. Budget for the setup period as well as the two-year test.

## What changes when you move from permit to licence

| Point | FinTech Experimental Permit (2021 Instructions, in force) | Capital market institution licence (CMIR, in force) |
|---|---|---|
| Term | Up to two years from commencement (s.10) | Ten years, renewed automatically while conditions are met (Art 7(g)) |
| Capital | Set by the CMA for each permit "if any" (s.6(B)(8)) | SAR 50 million for dealing and custody; SAR 20 million for managing investments and operating funds; one year of expected expenses for managing investments only, arranging and advising (Art 6(g)) |
| Legal form | A commercial entity in the Kingdom (s.7(A)(1)) | Restricted forms for dealing, custody and managing; any Saudi legal form for arranging and advising (Art 6(f)) |
| Decision clock | None stated | 30 days from the CMA's notice that the file is complete (Art 7(c)) |
| Registered people | Not listed in the Instructions; set through the CMA's conditions | CEO, CFO, compliance officer and MLRO at all times, plus an IT Officer for robo-advisory (Art 20(b)) |
| Clients and size | Client numbers and types, transaction sizes and promotions can be capped (s.6(B)) | The full rules for the licensed activity |

The restricted forms for dealing, custody and managing are a subsidiary of a local bank, a joint stock company, a subsidiary of a Saudi joint stock company in financial services, or a subsidiary of a foreign financial institution licensed under the Banking Control Law (the CMA's English text; check the Arabic before relying on the last one). The [capital market institution licence](@/licensing/capital-market-institution-licence/) page has the detail. A firm limited to managing investments, arranging or advising may delegate the CFO, compliance officer or MLRO function to an eligible external party (Art 20(e)).

The capital line has the largest consequences. A debt crowdfunding test that moves to an Arranging licence needs capital covering a year of expected expenses. A model that ends up needing a Dealing or Custody licence needs SAR 50 million and one of the restricted legal forms.

A separate May 2026 CMA draft ([CMA_N_4054](https://cma.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/NEWS/Pages/CMA_N_4054.aspx)) proposes lower dealing and custody capital and a SAR 2 million minimum for Arranging firms that hold client money in securities crowdfunding. Those figures are not in force.

## The 2026 draft would make the permit look more like a licence

On 26 April 2026 the CMA published a draft update to the Instructions ([CMA_N_4030](https://cma.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/NEWS/Pages/CMA_N_4030.aspx)) for 30 days, ending 27 May 2026. As of 25 November 2026 we have seen no approval on CMA Announcements, and the FinTech Lab page still links the 2021 Instructions, so the draft is not in force.

Reading the draft against the licence rules shows where it is heading:

- Its permit requirements in section 5(A)(1) to (4) repeat, almost word for word, the four applicant tests in CMIR Article 6(e): fit and proper for the kind and scale of the business, adequate expertise and resources, sufficient management, systems and controls, and qualified directors, officers and staff.
- A new section 12 on fit and proper copies the CMIR Article 9 criteria, including no fraud or dishonesty offence and no breach of securities laws.
- The CMA would decide within 60 days of its notice that a file is complete, against 30 days for a licence.
- It defines "graduation of fintech business models" and ties wider deployment after the test to that graduation.
- Where another regulator's licence is needed, the applicant would need that authority's approval or no-objection letter.
- After a permit is cancelled, the company would stay under CMA jurisdiction for two years, and it would have to give the CMA 30 days' notice before starting any Bankruptcy Law procedure.

If the draft is approved in this form, preparing for a permit would mean building much of a licence file at the start. Firms applying now are assessed under the 2021 text.

## Where the Arabic text says something different

The CMA's English regulations carry a note, on the cover of the [CMIR](https://cma.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Regulations/Documents/the_Capital_Market_Institutions_Regulations-en.pdf) for example, that the official text is in Arabic and the English is an unofficial translation. The Arabic FinTech Instructions differ from the English in four places that matter.

- **Stopping work for more than three months.** The English section 8(B)(3) says the CMA "will suspend the ExPermit". The Arabic says the CMA will cancel the permit and direct the company to apply again in later periods. The Arabic term is: بإلغاء التصريح. Plan on cancellation.
- **Capital.** Where the English section 6(B)(8) says "capital requirements (if any)", the Arabic says financial adequacy requirements, if any: متطلبات الكفاية المالية إن وجدت.
- **Cancellation for non-compliance.** The English section 11(A)(2) allows cancellation where a company cannot comply "at the end of the experimenting period". The Arabic has no such time limit.
- **The resolution number.** The English cover cites the 2021 update as Resolution 2-9-2021; the Arabic cover says 2-90-2021. Quote the Arabic number in Arabic filings.

## What to do now

1. Map your product to the five securities activities on our [CMA licences overview](@/licensing/cma-licences/). If your model is one the CMA has already moved into a licence, such as crowdfunding or robo-advisory, plan for a licence. For debt crowdfunding, the CMA still takes Lab applications that involve an innovative aspect requiring testing.
2. If no licence fits, write down why. The CMA may accept or reject a permit application, so that argument carries the file.
3. Budget for the gap between the Board decision and commencement. The five cases above ran from about 10 months to just over two years.
4. Choose the end licence before you start the test. The restricted legal form for Dealing, Custody and Managing, and the SAR 20 to 50 million capital for Dealing, Custody and Managing with Operating Funds, are hard to retrofit. Arranging and Advising accept any Saudi legal form.
5. Build the AML programme and the information security tests early. Both are commencement requirements under section 7, and registered compliance officer and MLRO roles follow at licence stage; see [the compliance function](@/compliance-function/).
6. Watch CMA Announcements for the approval of the April 2026 draft, which would change the entry tests and add a 60-day decision clock.

## Status and next milestone

- The 2021 FinTech Experimental Permit Instructions are in force.
- The Capital Market Institutions Regulations, amended by Board Resolution 2-3-2026, are in force.
- The April 2026 update to the Instructions is a draft; its consultation closed on 27 May 2026. The next step is a CMA Board approval announcement.
- Both registers change often, and we will update these counts when they do.
