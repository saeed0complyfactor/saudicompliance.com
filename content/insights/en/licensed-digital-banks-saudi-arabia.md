As of 23 November 2026, SAMA's register of licensed entities lists four digital banks: STC bank, Vision Bank, D360 Bank and EZ Bank. EZ Bank, the newest digital bank licence, is marked "Not yet operational". The four sit alongside 11 local banks and 24 branches of foreign banks, 39 banks in all, the same total SAMA gave when it announced EZ Bank's licence on 30 September 2025.

> **Key takeaways** SAMA has announced four digital bank licences since 2021, with capital at licensing between SAR 1.5 billion and SAR 2.5 billion. A licence and permission to operate are separate steps: D360 waited about 34 months from its licence announcement to SAMA's no objection to commence, and the bank licensed in 2021 as Saudi Digital Bank waited about 51 months on our reading. EZ Bank has been licensed for about 14 months and is not yet operating.

## The register on 23 November 2026

We read the Banks tab of [SAMA's Licensed Entities register](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/Supervision/LicenseEntities/Pages/default.aspx) on 23 November 2026, with all 39 entries on one page. It gives each bank's name, its category (local bank, digital bank or foreign bank branch), its activity, and its unified and commercial registration numbers.

| Category on the register | Banks | Marked "Not yet operational" |
|---|---|---|
| Local banks | 11 | 0 |
| Digital banks | 4 | 1 (EZ Bank) |
| Foreign bank branches | 24 | 2 (Trade Bank of Iraq, PT Bank Syariah Indonesia Tbk) |
| Total | 39 | 3 |

Source: SAMA Licensed Entities, Banks, read 23 November 2026.

When SAMA announced EZ Bank's licence, it put the total at 39 licensed banks, "including 15 Saudi banks and 24 foreign bank branches". The register's 11 local banks and 4 digital banks add up to those 15 Saudi banks. Fourteen months later, the total is unchanged.

The four digital banks appear on the register as follows.

| Digital bank | Unified number | Commercial registration | Activity and status on the register |
|---|---|---|---|
| STC bank | 7010933328 | 1010901344 | Banking Business |
| Vision Bank | 7032464351 | 1010850478 | Banking Business |
| D360 Bank | 7030588763 | 1010822737 | Banking Business |
| EZ Bank | Not shown | Not shown | Banking Business (Not yet operational) |

Only EZ Bank carries the "Not yet operational" mark.

## Four licences in three announcements, 2021 to 2025

Each digital bank licence was approved by the Council of Ministers and announced by SAMA. The dates below are the dates of SAMA's releases, which may differ from the dates of the decisions themselves.

| SAMA announcement | Bank | Who is behind it, per SAMA | Capital at licensing | SAMA no objection to commence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 22 Jun 2021 | STC bank | STC Pay, converted into a local digital bank | SAR 2.5 billion | No release found |
| 22 Jun 2021 | Saudi Digital Bank (Vision Bank, on our reading) | Companies and investors led by Abdul Rahman bin Saad Al-Rashed and Sons Company | SAR 1.5 billion | 15 Sep 2025, as Vision Bank |
| 15 Feb 2022 | D360 Bank | Individual and corporate investors including the Public Investment Fund, led by Derayah Financial Company | SAR 1.65 billion | 17 Dec 2024 |
| 30 Sep 2025 | EZ Bank | Not stated | SAR 2.5 billion | Not yet (register shows "Not yet operational") |

Sources: SAMA news releases of [22 June 2021](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-676.aspx), [15 February 2022](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-738.aspx), [17 December 2024](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-1061.aspx), [15 September 2025](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-1102.aspx) and [30 September 2025](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-1112.aspx). The Arabic versions ([22 June 2021](https://www.sama.gov.sa/ar-SA/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-676.aspx), [15 February 2022](https://www.sama.gov.sa/ar-SA/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-738.aspx), [17 December 2024](https://www.sama.gov.sa/ar-SA/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-1061.aspx), [15 September 2025](https://www.sama.gov.sa/ar-SA/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-1102.aspx), [30 September 2025](https://www.sama.gov.sa/ar-SA/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-1112.aspx)) give the same capital figures and dates.

Capital at licensing across the four banks comes to SAR 8.15 billion. These are the figures SAMA announced at approval. None of the releases states a minimum capital for digital banks (the [digital bank licence](@/licensing/digital-bank-licence/) page covers how SAMA sets it), and the release figures say nothing about capital held today.

The process started before the first approval. On 10 June 2021 SAMA [said it had finished studying the applications](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-672.aspx) of two local digital banks. It would submit the licensing requests to the higher authorities, using its powers under the Banking Control Law. Twelve days later it announced the Cabinet's approval of both. SAMA had issued its [Additional Licensing Guidelines and Criteria for Digital-Only Banks](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-501.aspx) on 24 February 2020, as a supplement to its general bank licensing guidelines.

## What happened to Saudi Digital Bank

The register does not list a bank called Saudi Digital Bank. SAMA's December 2024 release on D360 described it as "one of the three licensed digital banks", and the only digital bank licence SAMA has announced since is EZ Bank. Vision Bank is the only other digital bank on the register, so on our reading it is the bank SAMA licensed in June 2021 as Saudi Digital Bank. We did not find a SAMA release that states the change of name.

## STC bank's start date is not in a SAMA release

SAMA announced its no objection to commence for D360 and for Vision Bank. We found no equivalent release for STC bank. SAMA's [Financial Sector Development Program (FSDP) annual report for 2023](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/Documents/Financial_Sector_Development_Program_Annual_Report-2023-EN.pdf) says that in 2023 there was a pilot launch of "two out of three licensed digital banks (i.e. STC Bank and D360 bank)". It adds that the banks were working to be ready for a full launch in 2024. The [2024 report](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/Documents/Financial_Sector_Development_Program_Annual_Report-2024-EN.pdf) records D360's start of operations that year. The register today shows STC bank without a "not yet operational" mark. On our reading of the register and the FSDP reports, STC bank is operating, but SAMA has not published the date of its no objection in a news release.

## SAMA's older Digital Banks page is out of date

SAMA also keeps a separate [Digital Banks page](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/Supervision/LicenseEntities/Pages/DigitalBank.aspx). On 23 November 2026 it listed only two banks, "STC bank (Licensed - has not started yet)" and "Saudi Digital Bank (Licensed - has not started yet)". It does not show D360, Vision Bank or EZ Bank. Anyone checking a bank's status should use the main Licensed Entities register, which matches SAMA's own totals.

## From licence to no objection to commence took years

Using SAMA's announcement dates:

| Bank | Licence announced | No objection to commence | Elapsed |
|---|---|---|---|
| D360 Bank | 15 Feb 2022 | 17 Dec 2024 | About 34 months |
| Saudi Digital Bank / Vision Bank | 22 Jun 2021 | 15 Sep 2025 | About 51 months |
| EZ Bank | 30 Sep 2025 | Not yet as of 23 Nov 2026 | About 14 months so far |

Our arithmetic from SAMA's releases and register. STC bank is left out because we found no SAMA release on its no objection.

The gap is built into the process. When the Cabinet approved the first two banks, SAMA's Governor said SAMA would "finalize technical and operational requirements for the two banks to start their banking activities". Licensing and the permission to operate are two separate decisions. In both cases SAMA has dated, the no objection came 34 to 51 months after the licence announcement.

## Plan for two milestones and check the register before partnering

These points are our reading of SAMA's data. SAMA's releases do not comment on future digital bank licences, beyond its 2021 and 2022 statements that it would continue to receive or welcome banking licence applications.

1. A digital bank licence is a bank licence, approved by the Council of Ministers with capital in the billions of riyals. For products that do not take deposits, our guide to [e-money and payment institution licences](@/insights/e-money-vs-payment-institution-licence-saudi-arabia/) and the register analysis of [licensed finance companies](@/insights/sama-licensed-finance-companies-saudi-arabia/) cover those routes.
2. Plan for two milestones. The Cabinet approval is the headline, but full operations start only after SAMA's no objection to commence (D360 and STC bank ran pilots in 2023, according to the FSDP report). A business plan that assumes revenue soon after the licence announcement is out of line with both cases SAMA has dated.
3. Check partner status on the register before you sign. If you plan to partner with a digital bank for accounts, cards or settlement, confirm the bank is on the Licensed Entities register and is not marked "Not yet operational". EZ Bank is licensed but not yet operating as of 23 November 2026.
4. Record the register entry, unified number and the date you checked in your due diligence file, using the main register rather than the older Digital Banks page.
5. Watch for the next change. The next likely movement is EZ Bank's no objection to commence. SAMA announced that step for D360 and Vision Bank in news releases, so watch its News page. We will compare our snapshots and update this guide when the register changes.
